Community Guidelines

These rules exist so everyone — especially those overcoming social anxiety or seeking advice — can feel safe in both text and video.

Zero Tolerance: Adult Content

Any sexual, explicit, pornographic, or suggestive content is strictly forbidden in text messages, video, or behavior. This includes requests for such material. Violation results in an immediate permanent ban. PeerPath is not an adult platform.

Video Conduct

Camera and microphone are optional and under your full control. You may turn them off at any time. Appropriate clothing and respectful behavior are required on camera. No flashing, no inappropriate backgrounds or gestures. Text-only mode is always available.

Respect & Kindness

Treat every person with dignity. Many users are here to overcome fear of talking to others or to seek genuine advice. Mocking, bullying, discrimination, or pressure of any kind is not allowed.

Age Requirement

You must be 18 years of age or older to use PeerPath. By entering the platform you confirm this. We do not knowingly allow minors.

Reporting

If a conversation (text or video) becomes vulgar, harassing, or otherwise violates these guidelines, use the Report button immediately. We review reports and take action. False reports may also lead to restrictions.

Friend Requests

You may send a friend request only after a positive, respectful conversation. Do not pressure anyone. Friends lists are private and optional.

Enforcement

We use automated filters, user reports, and moderation review. Consequences range from temporary restrictions to permanent bans depending on severity. Our goal is a safe space for cross-cultural and intergenerational connection via text and video.
