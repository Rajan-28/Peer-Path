Full Safety First

Zero Tolerance for Harm

PeerPath is designed to be the opposite of toxic random chat platforms. Safety is the foundation — for both text and video.

No Adult Content

Strict ban on any sexual, explicit, or adult material in text or video. Automated filters + human review. Instant permanent ban.

You Control Camera

Camera and mic are optional. Turn them off anytime. Video is never required — text-only mode is always available.

One-Click Report

Every session has a visible Report button. Reports are reviewed quickly. Abusive users are removed.

18+ Age Gate

Only adults can join. We verify age intent at entry and enforce community standards strictly.

Friend Requests Only

No forced contact. You choose who stays in your network after a good conversation.

Clear Guidelines

Transparent community rules. Everyone knows what is expected — kindness, respect, zero harassment.
